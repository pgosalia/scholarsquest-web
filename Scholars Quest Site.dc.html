About us

A small practice, on purpose

Scholar's Quest is one-on-one tutoring in Math and English for Kindergarten through Grade 12, built around the belief that confidence comes before grades, not the other way round.

Founder portrait: Kathni Patel
The founder

Kathni Patel

I've spent more than four years working with children in Math and English, and the thing I keep coming back to is that no two students get stuck in the same place. So I teach to the student in front of me: their pace, their learning style, the specific thing that isn't landing, rather than to a fixed curriculum.

Homework, exam preparation, or a concept that has refused to click for a year: we work on whatever is actually in the way. Grades improve, but the change parents mention first is usually confidence.

Scholar's Quest has stayed deliberately small. I match every family myself, build the learning plan with you and your child, and read the session notes.

Questions parents ask

Draft answers. Correct anything that doesn't match how you actually run sessions.

How long is a session, and how often?
Sessions run 60 minutes. Most families book once or twice a week; test-prep students often go twice weekly in the two months before the exam.
What if we need to reschedule?
Reschedule or cancel with reasonable notice and there's no charge and no penalty. Illness and school events happen; we'd rather move the session than waste it.
How is a tutor matched to my child?
We match on subject and grade level first, then on temperament. A child who freezes up needs a different pace than one who races ahead. If the fit isn't right after a session or two, tell us and we'll change it.
What actually happens in the demo class?
A short diagnostic to see where your child is, then real work on something they're currently stuck on. It's a teaching session, not a sales call, and you get written notes afterwards.
How will I know it's working?
You get notes after every session covering what we worked on and what to practise, plus a progress summary you can compare against school reports.
Should I sit in on sessions?
You're welcome to, and for younger children it often helps for the first session. After that most students concentrate better on their own, and the notes keep you in the loop.

Privacy policy

Effective date: December 15, 2025

Scholar's Quest ("Scholar's Quest," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Scholar's Quest mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App.

1. Who this policy applies to

The App may be used by parents/guardians and students (including children), and by tutors and administrators. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child is using the App without your permission, contact us (Section 12).

2. Information we collect

Information you provide: account information (name and email address); profile information (optional bio, avatar, profile photo); tutoring and learning information (session details, assignment details, homework submissions and related notes); messages (chat messages and related metadata); support communications.

Files you upload: homework submissions and assignment attachments are stored using Cloudinary. We store file links and metadata (URL, filename, size, upload time) in our database so they can be displayed in the App.

Information collected automatically: device and app information (device type, OS version, app version), plus diagnostics and crash data used to improve reliability and security.

3. How we use information

To create and manage accounts and authenticate users; provide tutoring features (sessions, assignments, submissions and messaging); connect students and parents with assigned tutors and administrators; maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse; troubleshoot and improve the App; and communicate service-related updates.

4. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as needed: between tutoring participants (student/parent, tutor, and administrators where applicable) to deliver tutoring services; with service providers who help operate the App; for legal or safety reasons; and in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets.

5. Service providers

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and Cloudinary for file uploads and storage. These providers process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's privacy and parental consent

We take children's privacy seriously. In the United States, COPPA generally requires verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act requires verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's personal data, and defines a child as under 18.

Our current approach: we expect a parent, guardian, or authorized school/tutor administrator to create and manage accounts for children. If we learn we collected a child's personal information without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it or obtain valid consent. Parents and guardians may request to access, correct, or delete their child's personal information by contacting us (Section 12).

7. App Store Kids category

If distributed in Apple's Kids category, Apple imposes additional requirements and restrictions, including around third-party data collection, analytics and advertising.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary to provide the App and its features, maintain records for legitimate business needs, and comply with legal obligations. You may request account deletion (Section 10). Some information may be retained where required by law or for safety and security reasons.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and rights (Canada, US, India)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting us (Section 12). You may request deletion of your account and associated data; we may need to verify your identity first. If you are in India, you may have rights as a "Data Principal" under India's data protection law, including access, correction and grievance handling.

11. International data transfers

Because we use global service providers, your information may be stored or processed in countries other than where you live, including wherever Firebase and Cloudinary operate. By using the App, you understand your information may be transferred internationally.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, including parent and guardian requests, contact us at support@scholars.quest.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date above and post the revised policy in the App. If changes are significant, we may provide additional notice.
