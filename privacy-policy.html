Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 15, 2025

Scholar's Quest ("Scholar's Quest," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Scholar's Quest mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App.

1) Who This Policy Applies To

This App may be used by parents/guardians and students (including children), and tutors and administrators. If you are a parent/guardian and believe your child is using the App without your permission, contact us (Section 12).

2) Information We Collect

A. Information you provide: account information (name and email address); profile information (optional bio, avatar, and profile photo); tutoring and learning information (session details, assignment details, homework submissions, and related notes); messages (chat messages and related metadata); support communications.

B. Files you upload: if you upload files (e.g., homework submissions or assignment attachments), those files are stored using Cloudinary, and we store file links/metadata (URL, filename, size, upload time) in our database to display them in the App.

C. Information collected automatically: device and app information (device type, OS version, app version); diagnostics and crash data to improve reliability and security.

3) How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts and authenticate users; provide tutoring features (sessions, assignments, submissions, and messaging); connect students/parents with assigned tutors and administrators; maintain security and prevent fraud/abuse; troubleshoot and improve the App; and communicate service-related updates.

4) How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed: between tutoring participants (student/parent and tutor, and administrators where applicable) to deliver tutoring services; with service providers who help operate the App; for legal/safety reasons; and in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or sale of assets.

5) Service Providers

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and database services (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and Cloudinary for file uploads and storage. These providers process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

6) Children's Privacy and Parental/Guardian Consent

We take children's privacy seriously. In the United States, COPPA generally requires verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act requires verifiable consent of a parent/lawful guardian before processing a child's personal data, and defines a child as under 18.

How we handle this (V1 approach): we expect a parent/guardian (or authorized school/tutor admin) to create/manage accounts for children. If we learn we collected a child's personal information without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete the information or obtain valid consent. Parents/guardians may request to access, correct, or delete their child's personal information by contacting us (Section 12).

7) App Store Kids Category Note

If distributed in Apple's Kids category, Apple imposes additional requirements and restrictions (including around third-party data collection/analytics/ads).

8) Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary to provide the App and its features, maintain records for legitimate business needs, and comply with legal obligations. You may request account deletion (Section 10). Some information may be retained where required by law or for safety/security reasons.

9) Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10) Your Choices and Rights (Canada, US, India)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information by contacting us (Section 12). You may request deletion of your account and associated data; we may need to verify your identity first. If you are in India, you may have rights as a "Data Principal" under India's data protection law, including access, correction, and grievance handling.

11) International Data Transfers

Because we use global service providers, your information may be stored or processed in countries other than where you live (including where Firebase/Cloudinary operate). By using the App, you understand your information may be transferred internationally.

12) Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests (including parent/guardian requests), contact Scholar's Quest at support@scholars.quest — scholars.quest.

13) Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Effective date" above and post the revised policy in the App. If changes are significant, we may provide additional notice.
