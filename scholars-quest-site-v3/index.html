How it works

Three steps from first click to first session, usually inside a week.

Step one

Grade, subject, and what’s been hard lately. A few sentences is plenty.

Step two

Pick a time that fits

Weekday evenings and weekends included. Reschedule any time without penalty.

Step three

Meet your tutor, free

A real working session. Afterwards you get notes on what we saw and a plan, and you decide.
