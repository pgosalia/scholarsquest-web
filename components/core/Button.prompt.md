Pill-shaped CTA; pink `primary` for the single most important action on a view, teal `secondary` beside it.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg" pop iconRight="arrow-right">Book a free lesson</Button>
```

Variants: primary (pink), secondary (teal), outline, ghost, sun. `pop` adds the 4px ink sticker shadow — hero and pricing CTAs only.
